MHC class I presents 8–11-residue peptides to CD8+ T-cells

  • Cytosolic proteins → proteasome → short peptide fragments
  • Peptides loaded onto MHC class I in the ER
  • Displayed on the cell surface for TCR surveillance
  • CD8+ T-cells read the surface display and kill cells whose fragments look wrong

This is how immune surveillance sees inside cells it can't physically enter.

Why we care computationally — healthy vs tumour

  • Healthy cells → self peptides → immune tolerance
  • Tumour cells → neoantigens → cytotoxic T-cell recognition
  • Clinical angle: personalised cancer vaccines

If we can predict which peptides a patient's HLA will present, we can prioritise which neoantigens to put in a vaccine.

A ranking problem over 10²–10⁴ candidate peptides

  • Input: (peptide, HLA pseudo-sequence) pair
  • Output: BA (log-IC50) + EL (binding probability)
  • Deployed score: rank-averaged fusion of the two heads
  • Upstream of expensive experimental validation

Problem 2 — four head-to-head tests → Holm across the family of 4

  • Per-allele Wilcoxon within each row (84 alleles → 1 p-value)
  • Holm family-wise correction across the 4 Table 2 comparisons (m = 4)
  • Allele-clustered bootstrap CI on paired Δ

Only the NetMHCpan BA-head advantage (Δ = −0.0137, Holm p = 0.0017) survives. The pooled fusion win (raw p = 0.05) drops to Holm p = 0.15 and does not.

The three-condition superiority rule

① DeLong paired ROC

Pooled AUROC difference on the same rows, p < 0.05

② Per-allele Wilcoxon · Holm

Signed-rank over 84 alleles per row, Holm-adjusted across the family of 4

③ Allele-clustered bootstrap

Resample at the allele level, 95% CI on Δ excludes zero

If all three hold → we claim an asymmetry. If one or two → we report numbers and refuse to claim superiority. This is the paper's main methodological contribution.

DeepNeo-CL — ESM2-35M + cross-attention + two heads

  • 35M-param ESM2 trunk — frozen 2 epochs, then top-2 unfrozen (10× lower LR)
  • Peptide × HLA pseudo-sequence cross-attention + V37 physchem
  • BA head (censored log-IC50) · EL head (focal BCE)
  • 5-fold ensemble; deployed BA = batch-rank average

What the paper actually contributes

① Model

A compact 35M-param PLM ranker, trained on the public corpus, that matches the industrial baseline at the fused score.

② Evaluation contract

A leakage-aware, multiplicity-controlled protocol: exact decontamination + DeLong + Holm + bootstrap. Portable to any shared-corpus ranking task.

③ Failure-mode case studies

Two transferable traps:
· successor-corpus overlap (99.93%)
· transductive-CV collapse (0.862 → 0.442)

If there's one thing to remember, it's contribution ②. The model is a compact competitor; the methodology is what we hope people adopt.

MHC class I presents 8–11-residue peptides to CD8+ T-cells

  • Cytosolic proteins → proteasome → short peptide fragments
  • Peptides loaded onto MHC class I in the ER
  • Displayed on the cell surface for TCR surveillance
  • CD8+ T-cells read the surface display and kill cells whose fragments look wrong

This is how immune surveillance sees inside cells it can't physically enter.

Why we care computationally — healthy vs tumour

  • Healthy cells → self peptides → immune tolerance
  • Tumour cells → neoantigens → cytotoxic T-cell recognition
  • Clinical angle: personalised cancer vaccines

If we can predict which peptides a patient's HLA will present, we can prioritise which neoantigens to put in a vaccine.

A ranking problem over 10²–10⁴ candidate peptides

  • Input: (peptide, HLA pseudo-sequence) pair
  • Output: BA (log-IC50) + EL (binding probability)
  • Deployed score: rank-averaged fusion of the two heads
  • Upstream of expensive experimental validation

What this paper is — and what it isn't

What it is

A compact PLM trained on the official NetMHCpan-4.1 CV folds, evaluated under a disciplined leakage-aware + multiplicity-controlled protocol, with every number backed by a public SI JSON ledger.

What it isn't

A new state-of-the-art ranker.

A new statistical method.

A clinical result.

Target reader

A pMHC researcher about to publish a benchmark — this paper gives them a protocol to defend and a specific leakage ledger to cite.

The field already has NetMHCpan. The paper's value sits in the discipline of its evaluation and the receipts of its public ledger.

◐ MidThree-condition rule — standard tools, applied with discipline

① DeLong (1988)

Paired ROC on the same rows, p < 0.05

② Holm (1979) · Wilcoxon

Per-allele signed-rank over 84 alleles per row, Holm-adjusted across the family of 4 comparisons

③ Bootstrap (1979)

Resample at the allele level, 95% CI on Δ excludes zero

Nothing here is new statistics. The contribution is applying all three with discipline in a sub-field that routinely does pooled-AUROC-only comparisons. Under this contract, the only Holm-robust TransPHLA asymmetry is NetMHCpan's BA-head lead.

DeepNeo-CL — ESM2-35M + cross-attention + two heads

  • 35M-param ESM2 trunk — frozen 2 epochs, then top-2 unfrozen (10× lower LR)
  • Peptide × HLA pseudo-sequence cross-attention + V37 physchem
  • BA head: censored log-IC50 · EL head: focal BCE (γ = 2)
  • 5-fold ensemble on the official NetMHCpan-4.1 folds; deployed BA = batch-rank average

Efficient to retrain on commodity GPUs; same training exposure as NMP-4.1, so any residual gap can't be blamed on "you had more data".

Ablation — gain is distributed, not any single trick

  • Baseline: ESM2-35M peptide-only, AUROC 0.952
  • Deployment: SupCon v2 ensemble, AUROC 0.970
